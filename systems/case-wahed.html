Case study · Wahed

A new category, four regulators, one voice

A Shariah-compliant investing platform across the UK, US, Malaysia and UAE. A new category to most of the people it needed to reach, and every word clearing compliance in four markets.

The problem

Explain too little and nobody understands what they are buying. Explain too enthusiastically and you are misleading people about money.

What I did

  • Rebranded away from the fintech default. Real colour, past the blue and white every finance app settles on.
  • Defined the tone of voice, then wrote every surface to it. Website, app, journal, newsletters, ads and campaigns.
  • Put “capital at risk” on every caption. A brand that overstates returns has nothing left to sell.
  • Chose the faces deliberately. Khabib, Islam Makhachev, Paul Pogba, and Mufti Menk as brand ambassador.
  • Turned fandom into accounts. Fight tickets for people who opened one.

Where the leads came from

Top
The Muslim Money Experts podcast.
Middle
The journal and newsletter, built as a lead engine rather than brand filler.
Middle
Ambassador reach, into an audience written off as uninterested in investing.
Bottom
Account opening, with events as the closing incentive.
Brand awareness from 4.5% to 30%.

One voice across four regulated markets, every word cleared through US SEC compliance.

What to take from it

In a regulated category, compliance is the brand. Saying less, identically in every market, is what makes a new category feel safe enough to try.
