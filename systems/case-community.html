Case study · Ivory

Building the room before the product

A preventative health product, going into a market that did not know it had a problem. So we built the audience first.

The problem

Preventative products have no urgency. Nobody searches for a brain assessment.

What I did

  • Started with caregivers, not patients. People looking after someone with dementia.
  • Made the ask mutual. Bring someone who is also a caregiver. The invitation was the growth loop.
  • Gave first, for months. Sessions on sleep, habits and routine. A wellness community, not a waiting list.
  • Introduced the assessment last. Once the room had value without it.

Where the leads came from

Top
Caregivers bringing caregivers, so cost per member fell as it grew.
Middle
Webinars and habit sessions, which explained why screening matters.
Middle
An owned list, not a rented audience.
Bottom
The assessment, offered to a room that already trusted us.
3,000 members before the product shipped.

It kept generating assessment leads long after launch week.

What to take from it

A community is a lead engine you own outright. The discipline is resisting the urge to sell into it early.
